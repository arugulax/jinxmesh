This is the map the M9 draws, read from the same files. A saved area goes into a folder below /maps on the SD card, like any other piece. For another program, convert the file on your own computer with jmv-convert.py (Python 3, nothing to install): python3 jmv-convert.py area.jmv area.geojson, or .gpx, or .kml. Whole states are on the flasher page. Map data ODbL; elevation from the Terrain Tiles open dataset.
