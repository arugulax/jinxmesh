Flash your M9 from the browser

Install or update JinxMesh on an Elecrow ThinkNode M9 and load maps for your area. Nothing to install, and nothing about your device is sent anywhere.

This browser cannot talk to USB serial devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or flash with esptool as the install guide describes.
This page must be opened over https for the browser to allow USB access.
Step 1

Firmware

Loading the firmware list.

  1. Plug the M9 into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press Connect the M9 and pick its port (it shows as "USB Serial" or "CH340").
  3. Choose an update or a first install.

A first install wipes settings, channels, keys and chats, and is what a device coming from stock Meshtastic needs. An update keeps them. Only flash a ThinkNode M9: the page checks the chip, but not the board.

Step 2, optional

Maps for your area

Offline vector maps of the United States and Canada. Tick whole states, or open one and click only the pieces you need.

This browser cannot write to a folder you pick. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer for that, or use the download links.

SD card: put the M9's card in a card reader, press the button and pick the card itself. The maps go to /maps/state/ and /maps/province/; files already there are skipped. Cable: the card stays in the M9, which must be running JinxMesh and connected in step 1. It moves about 3.5 MB a minute, so it suits a piece or two, not a whole state.
