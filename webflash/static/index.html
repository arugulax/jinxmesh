Devices on this computer

Firmware from the server

Flashing uses the selected device. Keep this tab in front until it finishes. If the device does not answer, hold its BOOT button while plugging it in, then add it again.

Files for the device's SD card

Or a file from this computer
Folder /

Sends a file over the USB cable to the card of the selected device, and compares it afterwards. Slow but sure: about 3.5 MB a minute. A transfer that broke off goes on where it stopped. Needs JinxMesh firmware with the file transfer. "Save here" on a file of the card brings it to this computer; the Wardrive logs are one CSV file per day.

Logs over Bluetooth

Folder /apps/wardrive

Brings the wardrive and trip logs (the card's /apps folder, to read only) to this computer without the cable. The device asks for its pairing code the first time. Only one Bluetooth connection at a time: disconnect the phone app first. While this page is connected, "Send the log by Bluetooth" (hold OK in the Wardrive app of the device) brings the log here without a click. Needs JinxMesh firmware 2.8.3 from 2026-11-21 on and a browser with Web Bluetooth (Chrome, Edge). About 10 kB a second.

Navigate: a destination or a route

Connect by Bluetooth above first.

One place a line: latitude, longitude in degrees with a point, then a name. One line is a destination; several are the stops of a route in their order (at most 1024; a long route takes about a minute to send), which the device follows one after the other and keeps on its card in /apps/routes. The route itself is found on the device, from where it is, over the roads and trails of /geo/roads.bin. The phone app or the Meshtastic web client can also send a waypoint: on the device it is under Navigate, A waypoint.

Tunes for the buzzer

Press a note to hear and add it: each row is one octave of the scale, low at the top (octaves 4 to 7 are what a tune can hold), or edit the text (RTTTL) directly. The buzzer plays one note at a time. Saved tunes go to /tones on the card and turn up in Settings, Sounds, in every list of tones. A tune for "Notification tone" may have 230 characters at most, the others 399.
